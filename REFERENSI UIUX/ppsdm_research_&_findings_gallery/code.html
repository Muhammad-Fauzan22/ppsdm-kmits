Cognitive Development Oct 2023

The Impact of Remote Learning on Cognitive Development

This study analyzes the longitudinal effects of remote learning modalities on student cognitive flexibility and problem-solving skills across three academic years. Findings suggest a bimodal distribution in adaptability.

Dr. Sarah Lin, et al.

Institutional Resilience Aug 2023

Institutional Resilience in Post-Pandemic Cohorts

An examination of structural and social support systems implemented in 2021 and their correlation with student retention rates. Data indicates a significant positive correlation between early mentorship programs and long-term success.

Prof. James Chen
